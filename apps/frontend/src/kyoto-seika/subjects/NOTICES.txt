Kyoto Seika Subjects (subjects.json)

The list of subjects, with their readings and English words, is licensed under the
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

It is built from:

- JMdict, by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG): words, readings and
  English words. CC BY-SA 4.0.
  https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project
  https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
- Japanese Wiktionary, through kaikki.org: to check each word's sense while choosing; nothing
  from it ships. CC BY-SA 4.0.
  https://kaikki.org/jawiktionary/
- Princeton WordNet 3.0: categories, to sort the subjects into kinds; nothing from it ships. Its
  licence follows.
- Japanese WordNet 1.1, NICT: links from words to WordNet's senses, for choosing only. Its notice
  follows. https://bond-lab.github.io/wnja/
- The JLPT lists by Jonathan Waller (http://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/, CC BY), keyed to JMdict by
  https://github.com/stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab (CC BY-SA 4.0): levels, for choosing only.
- KANJIDIC2, EDRDG: the school grade of each kanji, for choosing only. CC BY-SA 4.0.
- wordfreq (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq): word frequency, for choosing only. Data CC BY-SA 4.0.

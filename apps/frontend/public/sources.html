Sources

The entrance exam mode deals its subjects from a list built from the sources below. The list, with its readings and English words, is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Where the subjects come from

  • JMdict, by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG): words, readings and English words. CC BY-SA 4.0, under the EDRDG licence.
  • KANJIDIC2, EDRDG: each kanji's school grade, for choosing only. CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Japanese Wiktionary, through kaikki.org: to check each word's sense while choosing; nothing from it ships. CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Princeton WordNet 3.0: categories, to sort the subjects into kinds; nothing from it ships. Its licence is below.
  • Japanese WordNet 1.1, NICT: links from words to WordNet's senses, for choosing only. Its notice is below.
  • The JLPT lists by Jonathan Waller (http://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/, CC BY), keyed to JMdict by stephenmk's yomitan-jlpt-vocab (CC BY-SA 4.0): levels, for choosing only.
  • wordfreq: word frequency, for choosing only. Data CC BY-SA 4.0.

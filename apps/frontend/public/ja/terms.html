ギレン・ザビ、ガルマ・ザビ国葬での演説（『機動戦士ガンダム』第12話「ジオンの脅威」、1979年）

Citizens of the Sticker Board!

We have lost our Terms of Use.

They were to run forty pages. They would have defined “you,” “us” and “the Service.” They would have reserved every right, disclaimed every warranty and sent every dispute to arbitration in a city you have never visited. They were never written, and this hackathon will end before they are.

But does this mean defeat? No! It is the beginning!

For too long, the Federation of Fine Print has ruled over the apps you love. Each one asks you to scroll past a wall of clauses and tap “I agree,” and it calls that consent. The Federation draws nothing. It seals nothing. It has never once given a sticker to a friend.

We ask far less of you. Draw one sticker before the timer runs out. Seal it, so that no hand, not even yours, can change it again. Put it in a gift bag and send it through LINE to one friend. When they pull the tab, they will play for its heart and send back Gratitude. Gratitude cannot be bought. It is earned with the fingertip, one hit at a time.

Our Terms of Use have fallen. Why?

Somewhere, in a bar, the Red Comet raises his glass: “Because it was a hackathon.”

Artists! Turn your sorrow into stickers! Rise, and take up your finger, or your Pencil if you have one. Let the Federation draft its clauses. We will be sticking things on the backs of laptops.

Until the Terms are written, these are the Terms:

  • Draw your own stickers.
  • A sealed sticker is final.
  • A gift opens once, for the friend it was sent to.
  • Give Gratitude generously.

Sieg Sticker!

The crowd, who came for the other speech: Sieg Zeon! Sieg Zeon! Sieg Zeon!

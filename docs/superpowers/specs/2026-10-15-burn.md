# Burn

Design approved by ad0ll, 2026-10-15. Burning throws away your own work for good: a sticker in progress from the seal sheet, to move on to your next ticket, or a sealed sticker you drew from its detail, so it no longer shows anywhere.

## Burning a sticker in progress

- The seal sheet offers Burn whenever the sticker has ink or its preview couldn't be checked, whether the check key or 0:00 opened it. At 0:00 the choice is Seal or Burn.
- Nothing goes to the server or Sui. The ticket stays spent: its `ticket_uses` row keeps no sticker, as an abandoned sheet's does. The device wipes its kept copy and the start screen comes next, as Start a new sticker does today; the next ticket is spent only when the person starts drawing.
- Burn replaces every control that throws ink away today:
  - Start a new sticker under the seal sheet's failed preview at 0:00;
  - Start a new sticker in the drawing screen's corner, after a seal fails at 0:00 and can't have landed;
  - the kept drawing recovery sheet's three discards.
- Start a new sticker stays under an empty preview at 0:00, where nothing would burn. The seal sheet's line "Starting a new sticker discards this drawing." goes.
- Never offered while a seal may have reached the server, the rule that gates Start a new sticker today.
- It was never sealed, so it counts toward neither stickers made nor the streak.

## Burning a sealed sticker

**Who:** its Original Artist, from its detail, beside Mark 18+, while they hold it, it has no Transfer Trail, and no gift holds it. A sticker in a gift shows Take it out instead.

**Gone, for everyone, from:**

- the sticker board, in both layouts;
- the sticker tray, including its spot on the sticker sheet, which a sticker given away keeps;
- Explore's pile;
- its detail and timelapse;
- Giving, including the sticker picker on someone else's board.

**Images:** the box stops serving the files that show its drawing, unless an unburned sticker shows the same drawing; they then follow that sticker's 18+ mark. A burn that takes a drawing out of public view makes its CDN purge due, run and retried as a mark's is (`apps/api/src/stickers/nsfwDrawing.ts`). A veil an 18+ mark made stays public, as every veil does, and so do its mask and foil files, which show only its cut, as an 18+ sticker's do.

**Unchanged:**

- The Sui object, in its holder's account; a wallet or Sui explorer shows a broken image.
- Its rows, image files and timelapse.
- Its number, which is never reused.
- User Stats: stickers made and both streaks still count it, so a burn never breaks a streak after the fact.
- The leaderboards.
- A mint still in flight lands as usual.

**Refusals** stay on the detail with their reason:

- 404 `sticker_not_found`: there's no such sticker, or it's burned.
- 403 `not_original_artist`.
- 403 `not_yours`: someone else holds it.
- 409 `has_transfer_trail` (new).
- 409 `gift_in_transit`: a gift holds it.

## The hold

- One control for every burn. Holding it sets fire to the sticker's edge; letting go early puts the fire out, and nothing burns. Holding Space or Enter does the same.
- Screen readers announce "hold to burn"; VoiceOver's double-tap and hold works.
- Under reduced motion, a plain fill replaces the fire.
- A long press opens no callout, text selection or magnifier, in Safari, LINE or Chrome.
- New to the design system. Its look, hold time and fire, including on the recovery sheet, which shows no sticker, are mocked with impeccable over the running app, in both languages and WebKit, and approved before it's built. Its DESIGN.md entry then goes to ad0ll as a question.

## Restoring

The operator runs a script on the box. `list` shows each burned sticker: its number, Original Artist, holder and when it burned. `restore <number>` puts one back in its holder's sticker tray, as the same Sui object. It ships with the API. Fastly keeps no image error, so its images serve again at once.

An admin screen is a separate project: the API's only operator check is person switching's.

## API and data

- `stickers.burned_at`: when it burned, or null. Added last in the table.
- `POST /api/stickers/:stickerId/burn`, in `apps/api/src/stickers/burn.ts`: checks and burns in one transaction, taking the sticker off both layouts. Answers 204, and 204 again to its holder once it's burned, so a retry after a lost answer is safe.
- One condition keeps burned stickers out of every read that shows a sticker: the board, the tray, the pile, the detail, the timelapse, Giving and the images. Each read has a test.
- A placement save still waiting for a burned sticker is dropped, not retried.

## Testing

- API: each refusal; a repeat burn; each read leaving a burned sticker out; a shared drawing still served under its 18+ rules; a burn making a purge due; User Stats still counting it; `list` and `restore`.
- Frontend unit: the hold burns only after its full time, cancels on release, holds by keyboard, and turns plain under reduced motion; where the seal sheet and the detail offer Burn.
- Playwright, in Chromium and WebKit: burn a sticker in progress at the seal sheet and land on the start screen, the ticket still spent; burn a sealed sticker and find it gone from the board, the tray and Explore.
- `pnpm --filter frontend test:ipad-safari`: the hold in real iPad Safari, with no callout, selection or magnifier.
- Before done: a code review, an impeccable critique, and the performance recorder on the fire, on a phone and an iPad.

## Out of scope

- Destroying the Sui object, which would need the stickers package's first upgrade.
- An admin screen.
- Undoing a burn yourself.
- Burning a sticker someone else drew.

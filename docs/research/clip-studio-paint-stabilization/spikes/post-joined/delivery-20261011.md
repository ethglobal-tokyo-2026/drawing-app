## Public finding and correction

The first public WebKit light-pressure worker comparison differed from Node in
four interior widths by one ULP; every compared coordinate, time, and endpoint
width agreed. The [saved failure](evidence/production-smoke-2026-10-11T23-34-53.439Z-9573597d-730b-408d-9f4d-3029ad408111/report.json)
is preserved. An [isolated causal probe](evidence/attribute-distance-20261011/README.md)
evaluated the exact deployed adapter in Node, Chromium, and WebKit and traced
the differences to its two `Math.hypot` calls.

The fix uses explicitly ordered scaled distance arithmetic only for Croquis's
normalized width/time transfer. Recovered CSP geometry and scalar equations
are unchanged. The isolated replacement matched all 22 segment distances,
22 cumulative distances, and 56 returned fields across its two cases in all
three runtimes. Cross-engine regressions failed before the production change
and passed afterward; this is bounded evidence, not a claim about every browser
and every numerical input.

## Limits

This is the selected ordinary open-path profile with Bézier off. Cache/copy
selection is not independently replayed; endpoint replay receives its observed
resolved configuration. The joined mouse captures have pressure one. The
shortest reaches modeled evaluator-dependent decisions and retains both
endpoints; native endpoint deletion and variable-pressure joined behavior remain
unobserved. Browser/OS acquisition, native brush rendering, physical iPad
performance, and natural pen feel are not established by these comparisons.

## Design review (Assessment A)

### Design specificity verdict

Authored for Croquis, not interchangeable. The ticket is the app's own object, made of the app's own materials: perforated stock in the key's colors, a used ticket that keeps the cut line of the sticker it became (the out-of-tickets stubs in `sheet-2-cards-en390.png`), a reserve ticket that "wears the stickers' resin", and stubs slid behind the Draw key like a hand of cards. Copy has a voice ("New at 12:00 AM", "Reserve tickets never expire.", "Today’s daily tickets are used."). The Shop's swatches are made of the product's real parts (the newest sticker under a coming laminate, brushes painted by the drawing screen's own `paintStroke`). The checkout's pack list is the one generic pattern, and it is right to be plain. Where the world thins out is failure: the payment-failure cards fall back to a stock dialog with a raw exception, and the shortage line sends the person off to "add JPYC" with no way there.

### Cognitive load

Checklist failures: 2 of 8 (moderate). **Minimal choices:** the checkout has seven controls at its decision point (four packs, Pay, the purchases toggle, Not now) and the "tickets not added" card has five (Add the tickets, Copy, Back to the packs, Not now, plus the payment ID to read). **Working memory:** the shortage line asks the person to leave the card, find their Sui address elsewhere (the stat board) and come back. Decision points over four options: those two cards. The out-of-tickets, reserve-ask and spend-failure cards each have two or three controls and one key.

### Emotional journey

- Peaks: three yellow stubs behind the key; the reserve ticket's star; the "3 reserve tickets added" card with the ticket's badge on the new total (`sheet-5b-checkout-en-b.png` panel 2).
- Valleys: "Out of tickets for today" is handled with care: the free path is the key, buying is label stock, the refill line is bold Ink with a countdown, and there is no lecture (`sheet-2-cards-en390.png` panel 1). The deep valley is a failed payment: a stock title over a wall of raw text (`sheet-5-checkout-en-a.png` panel 4), and the shortage line, which sends a first-timer somewhere the app doesn't lead.
- Reassurance at high stakes: strong for "paid but not added" ("won’t charge you twice"); absent while money moves ("Paying…" says nothing) and on timeout ("Check your JPYC balance before trying again" hands the check to the person).

### Strengths

1. **The Ticket Rule made visible.** One helper decides what every ticket picture shows, so the key, the cards and the Shop never disagree, and a used ticket keeps the outline of its sticker: scarcity felt, not explained.
2. **The out-of-tickets card asks nothing of you.** Back to my board is the key, buying is quieter label stock, the refill time is plain, and when the refill lands the card turns over in place into Draw.
3. **A payment in limbo is treated as the person's money.** Kept per person on the phone, retry-safe, reopened on its own card, with the ID and a Copy label.

### Priority issues

Why it matters: it is the moment the artist most needs the app to be calm and plain, and the audience is the one that reads any crypto residue as a racket. Fix: one catalog line per known failure in the world's voice, raw detail in fine print with Copy; on a slow confirmation, "Your payment is on its way; we'll add the tickets" and keep the payment.

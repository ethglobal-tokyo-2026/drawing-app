## Audit (technical)

Proposed scores for this slice (0–4):

- **A11y 2**: the own board's keyboard model is strong (roving Tab stop, arrows, move/turn/resize keys, Escape, hint read by screen readers, zero running animations under reduced motion), but someone else's board leaves Explore reachable underneath with focus stranded behind it, and its sticker menu is out of focus order and mis-roled (both measured).
- **Performance 3**: the light and every touch gesture are cheap (measured), but the resin sway is 85% of the board's idle main-thread time, keeps running under a turned board, and keyboard moves save once per key press.
- **Theming 3**: tokens used throughout; a handful of hard-coded rgba/hex, two off-scale 13.5px sizes, and one white-on-pink mark against the Ink-On-Color Rule.
- **Responsive 3**: 375×591, Japanese and a 32-character name all fit (measured); small misses (Explore chip touch area 42px, fixed 196px failure card, guessed menu geometry).
- **Integrity 3**: coherent and product-specific; dead glow code for a feature PRODUCT.md lists as not built, an undocumented lifted corner, a failure card drawn under cached stickers, and console-only errors.

### Positives (what to keep)

- The own board's keyboard model: one Tab stop, focus alone doesn't select, arrows walk stickers in reading order or move the selected one, `[` `]` turn, `-` `=` resize, Delete stows, Escape lets go, and the toolbar is the next Tab stop after its sticker (measured order: name, Draw, sticker, Give, View, Remove, zipper, tabs). The hint is read by screen readers via `aria-describedby`.
- Touch: handle and knob touch areas measure 44px (22px box reaches ±21px), a drag, resize, turn and two-finger pinch each save once, the pinch clamps at `S_MAX` (281px on a 390px board), no unsaved alert (script 09).
- Reduced motion keeps meaning: zero running animations, foil and resin still, chips still shown (script 09 `running:{}`).
- The light is cheap: 120 pointer moves cost 23 ms of script and frames stayed at or under 9.4 ms (headless, dev); its listeners exist only while a board holds the light (`light.ts:179-191`).
- A failed placement save shows in the UI with the reason and Try again (`StickerBoard.tsx:771-789`); the last-board cache and board-first assembly keep returning visits instant; the skeleton has one `role="status"` line.
- BoardFlip marks the turned-away face `inert` (`BoardFlip.tsx:134,138`).
- Every string is in the catalog in both languages; Japanese fits at 375×591 (toolbar 263px, chip 80px, menu 230px); sticker labels carry No., time, artist and place in reading order.

## Design review (Assessment A)

### Design specificity verdict

Authored for Croquis, not interchangeable. The front of your board is a シール帳 page: die-cut stickers with baked resin and a moving specular, a holo foil band that marks someone else's hand, a real zipper down the right edge, a Draw key whose used-ticket backing is printed "NEW AT 12:00 AM", a dashed die-cut spot on the empty page. Nothing here could be pasted into another product. The generic parts are the ones outside the world: the skeleton's four grey blobs, the failure card, and someone else's board, which trades the toolbar and chip greeting for a plain popover menu and shows foil without ever saying what it means.

### Cognitive load

- Checklist failures: hidden navigation (the name turns the board over; it looks like a label, no chevron or "stats" cue); too many implicit modes on one tap (select, open, deselect depend on what was selected before).
- Around a selected sticker: 4 corner handles, a knob, a toolbar of 3 actions and sometimes an artist chip; all appear together but each is spatially separate, so it holds up. Decision points over 4 options: none on the front.
- Someone else's board asks the same question with a different control (menu, not toolbar), so what you learn on your own board doesn't transfer.

### Emotional journey

- Peaks: the first sticker landing (stick, gloss sweep), the foil chips greeting your friend's name, the Draw key's hop on an empty board (four loops, then quiet: measured).
- Valleys: no tickets left (a permanent printed "NEW AT 12:00 AM" beside Draw is honest but never goes away until midnight), a load failure with a cached board (text under stickers), losing your place in Explore after visiting a board.
- Reassurance at stakes: Remove peels the sticker to the tray and marks where it was; a failed save says which sticker and why; Give and Receive are other lanes.

### Strengths

1. The material: resin, foil, zipper and the hop-and-ring first visit make the board feel like an object you own (screenshots 01, 03, 08).
2. The own board treats touch, keyboard and reduced motion as first-class: 44px targets, one save per gesture, a full key map, still-but-complete reduced motion.
3. The empty state is one page with one call to action: dashed spot, a printed nudge, the key that hops (screenshot 08).

### Priority issues

- **[P1] Someone else's board is the weaker twin of yours** — What: a second, hand-rolled menu, Explore left live underneath, no roving tab stop, no chip greeting. Why it matters: visiting friends' boards is the product's social loop and Offer for it lives only there; keyboard and VoiceOver users can barely operate it. Fix: reuse the own board's toolbar, focus and chip pattern; make Explore inert while it's open. Command: /impeccable harden
- **[P2] The gestures are learned by accident** — What: nothing after the first sticker teaches tap to select, tap again to open, tap bare board to let go, drag onto the zipper to stow, or that your name flips the board. Why: a first-timer's board becomes a static page; the stat board (where Gratitude lives) hides behind an unlabelled label. Fix: a one-time hint on the first selected sticker; a chevron or "stats" cue on the name. Command: /impeccable onboard
- **[P2] The failure state is the least designed state** — What: a fixed 236×196 card under cached stickers with raw reason text. Why: a returning person meets it exactly when the cache makes everything else look fine. Fix: raise it, size it to its reason, say what to do. Command: /impeccable harden
- **[P2] Foil's meaning is spoken for three seconds, again and again** — What: chips replay on every return to your board, never on a visited board, and are the only place "someone else drew this" is explained. Why: repeats become noise, and visitors never get the greeting. Fix: once per app open on your board, and on visited boards, with the same rule written in DESIGN.md. Command: /impeccable clarify
- **[P3] An empty board with a full tray says "Stickers you make or receive land here."** — What: the note shows whenever no sticker is on the board, even when the tray holds many (`StickerBoard.tsx:683-688`, `firstVisit` is separate at :615). Why: the useful next step is the zipper. Fix: a tray-aware line. Command: /impeccable clarify (code read; not seen on screen)

### Persona red flags

- **Casey (one-handed, interrupted)**: Draw sits at the bottom left and the zipper on the right edge, both in thumb reach; every gesture saves as it ends and the last board is cached, so interruption is safe. Red flags: pinch needs two fingers (the corner handles are the one-hand route, but sit 10px outside the sticker, near the screen edge for stickers at the sides); the name that flips the board is top left, the farthest reach for a right thumb.
- **Jordan (first-timer)**: the empty board is clear (screenshot 08). Then nothing explains dragging, resizing, the lifted corner on the newest sticker, or that the name button opens stats; the foil chips disappear after 3 s.
- **Sam (VoiceOver/keyboard, zoom)**: your board is good; someone else's is not (P1s above); no way to arrange without dragging or a hardware keyboard; selection isn't announced when a tap selects a sticker.
- **Friend-first artist**: the foil chips naming a friend are the warmest moment on the board; the board front has no numbers to compete with.
- **Gamer artist**: nothing on the front points to Gratitude or ranks (it lives on the stat board, behind the name).
- **Artist suspicious of crypto**: no chain words, prices or wallet terms anywhere on the front, in either language.

### Minor observations

- On first load the Draw key label and the avatar letter render in a fallback face for about a second before Dela Gothic One arrives (screenshots 01 vs 03; dev server, may differ in production).
- The toolbar and someone else's menu cover neighbouring stickers (screenshots 03, 05); acceptable, but a nudge toward the emptier side would help.
- The skeleton's four blobs are generic grey circles, and the zipper pops in after stickers (screenshot 09).
- Deep link `/@Cork_long_handle_for_the_byline1` opened Explore, not the board, while `/@board-owner` worked (entry lane; unverified cause).
- The Draw key's accessible name keeps its visible word in both languages ("Draw a new sticker: …", 「新しいシールをかく：…」), so Label in Name passes.

### Questions to consider

- Should visiting someone's board greet their foil stickers the way yours does, since that's where you meet other people's hands?
- Does the stat board need a visible cue on the name, given DESIGN.md says nothing is hidden behind a second place?
- Should the ambient sway only run on stickers being looked at (pointer near, just placed) instead of all of them, all the time?
- Could a selected sticker's toolbar carry a small "Arrange" row (nudge, size, turn) so touch-only and VoiceOver people get the same control as the keyboard?

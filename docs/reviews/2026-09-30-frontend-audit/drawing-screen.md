### Positives (what to keep)

- **The pointer path is clean.** Pointer events only queue samples; one animation frame paints the new segments; nothing goes through React. Measured with 120 touch samples per run at 1x, 4x and 6x CPU throttle: input to the frame that paints it (JS side) mean 14.9, 15.1 and 15.0 ms, p95 16.7, 17.0 and 17.0 ms, max 17.2, 17.8 and 21.3 ms; 0 of about 390 frames over 20 ms; no long tasks; canvas `fill` calls averaged 0.01 to 0.04 ms (`out-main.json` latencyTouch). WebKit 26.5: mean 8.0, p95 16, max 17 ms at 16.7 ms frames (`out-webkit.json` latency). The seal's cut runs in a worker: a 451,921-byte request left 450 ms after the second tap, no long tasks, and the whole seal took about 4.1 s in Chromium and 4.15 s in WebKit. Encoding and gzipping a synthetic 75,000-point timelapse took 9 ms and 5 ms.
- **Strokes survive.** A reload restores the strokes, the color, the ticket and the time drawn, paused, with "Picked up where you left off", and spends no second ticket (Chromium and WebKit; `out-main.json` reload extraSpends 0). A `pointercancel` after 4px keeps the stroke. Two-finger tap equals the Undo button; three-finger tap redoes (`out-nsfw.json` threeFingerRedo). An armed seal key disarms at 2.5 s (chip off and label restored at 2.8 s). The clock resumes and the sheet takes ink after a failed seal.
- **Feedback ladder.** The seal label appears at 1.7 s ("Sealing your sticker..."), gains "It can take up to half a minute." at 10.7 s and "It's taking longer than usual." at 30.7 s, exactly as DESIGN.md says; the sealed card moves focus to Keep drawing when it's ready; every hold shows one PAUSED tag and the description names the reason (color, smoothing, size).
- **Keyboard and names.** Toolbar with roving tabindex and arrow keys, `aria-pressed`, `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`, sliders with `aria-valuetext`, a `role="switch"` for 18+, a modal dialog with a focus trap for the sealed card, the whole screen `inert` when covered, 2px Ink focus rings on every stop I tabbed (`out-main.json` tabOrder). Ink on Tomato (timer's last ten seconds) 5.61:1, Ink on Seal Yellow 12.64:1, Graphite on Liner 4.77:1 (`out-verify.json` contrast).

## Design review (Assessment A)

### Design specificity verdict

Authored for Croquis, not interchangeable. The timer is a yellow dot sticker slapped on at -4° that grows a white label-tape "PAUSED" for every hold; hints and the sealing status are the same white label stock at -2°; the seal is a round keycap check that breathes when armed; the brush size is a tip-shaped thumb with a ghost of the real tip in mid-sheet; the ceremony is a Seal Yellow blade circling a die-cut before resin pours and the sticker peels onto a backing card whose ticket stubs keep the outline of the stickers they became. No Procreate-grey chrome, no gradient, no decorative glass. The canvas stays nearly bare, as PRODUCT.md asks. Where it slips into the category default is the color sheet (a standard swatch grid, pad and slider) and the error chip (developer text on a label), which are the two least authored surfaces.

### Cognitive load

Checklist failures: 1 of 8 (low). Minimal choices: the Color sheet offers up to 8 recents, 40 swatches, a hue pad and a brightness bar at once (four ways to pick a color, more than 4 options at the decision point). Everything else holds: one focus (the sheet), chunking (tools, sizes, history in separate corners), visual hierarchy (the yellow check is the one key). Decision points with more than 4 options: the Color sheet only; the tool strip is exactly 5 tiles.

### Emotional journey

Peaks: the die-cut and resin pour, the peel onto the card, the sealed card with used stubs, and Keep drawing's hand-over (the clock back at 3:00 under the veil, the sheet never bare). Valleys: the wait for the server (up to 60 s, a looping blade and three labels, no exit), the failed-seal chip (a block of developer text where the artist expects reassurance), and time-up on a bad connection (an unexplained frozen 0:00). Reassurance at high-stakes moments is good where it exists: "Picked up where you left off" after a reload, the clock restarting after a failed seal; it is missing at the two moments the sticker is most at risk (a failed seal and a failed time-up seal) because neither says "your sticker is safe here".

### Strengths

1. **The timer explains itself.** One white PAUSED tag for every reason the clock holds (tap, page hidden, color sheet, Smoothing bar, finger on the rail), a hint that points at the dot when a stroke lands on a paused sheet, and the same words for screen readers. An artist never wonders why the clock stopped.
2. **The ghost tip.** Holding the size rail shows the brush's real size in the middle of the sheet in the current color, with the px number beside the thumb, so the artist sizes the brush by looking at the paper, not at the rail (m03-size-held.png).
3. **Persistence that feels safe.** A reload brings back every stroke, the color and the ticket, paused and labelled, without spending again; a failed seal returns to the sheet with the clock running.

### Priority issues

1. **[P2] "Time's up" isn't a state.** What: a failed seal at 0:00 returns to a live sheet with a frozen 0:00, and the chip doesn't mention time. Why it matters: it breaks the three-minute promise the product is built on and confuses the honest artist who lost signal at the deadline. Fix: lock the sheet, say "Time's up" and offer only Seal again. Command: /impeccable harden.
2. **[P2] The failure chip is the worst copy on the screen.** What: 10 lines of developer text at 13px over the art, in Japanese with an English request line. Why: it lands when trust is lowest. Fix: one short line, detail to the developer slip. Command: /impeccable clarify.
3. **[P2] Two touches change state without saying so.** What: a stroke that starts on the rail's band resizes the brush and draws nothing; a tap that closes Smoothing draws a dot. Why: both read as the app misbehaving. Fix: the rail takes only its thumb; the bar closes without reaching the engine. Command: /impeccable harden.
4. **[P2] An irreversible mark sits on a casual control.** What: the 18+ pill, 30px, 6px above the seal key, unrestated at seal. Why: a mis-tap silently makes a friend's gift unreceivable for anyone not verified as an adult. Fix: state it on the armed chip and pad the target. Command: /impeccable harden.
5. **[P3] The Color sheet covers the art.** What: 71% of a 591px screen. Why: colors are chosen against the sticker. Fix: a compact sheet or a preview strip on short screens. Command: /impeccable layout.

### Persona red flags

- **Casey (one-handed, interrupted):** the timer, all five tools and the Color/Smoothing entry points are in the top 60px, the far end of the thumb's reach on a 741px phone, while undo, redo and the seal key sit low; the pause is top-left, opposite the thumb. Interruptions are handled well (hidden page holds the clock, reload restores paused, `pointercancel` keeps a stroke), but a stroke in progress when the page is killed is never committed (unverified on device).
- **Jordan (first-timer):** the check key has no visible word ("Seal: tap twice" exists only for screen readers); the chip after the first tap explains the second. Two- and three-finger taps and the 18+ pill are never introduced. "Starts when you draw" is helpful and goes after three visits.
- **Sam (VoiceOver, keyboard, zoom):** names, roles, valuetext and focus order are good; freehand drawing itself has no keyboard or VoiceOver equivalent (inherent to the task; the tools and shortcuts are reachable); no low-time announcement; the 18+ switch's on state is 3.07:1.
- **Friend-first artist:** the ceremony and Keep drawing are the reward loop and work; the 18+ mis-tap and a bad-connection failure are the two ways to feel punished for a friendly sticker.
- **Gamer artist chasing clout:** the 3-minute clock is the game; the untimed sheet after a failed 0:00 seal is a loophole they will find (verified, see P2).
- **Artist suspicious of crypto:** this screen never says NFT, chain, wallet or token; the only technical words are in the failure chip (`POST /api/stickers`), which reads as engineer text, not as anything financial.

### Minor observations

- Restoring a sheet brings back the ink and the color but not the brush size or Smoothing: after the reload in m13-reload.png the rail reads 7px again.

Fix meaning before polishing tone

01

Gift messages promise the wrong restriction

Several messages say a gift opens once, belongs only to the intended friend, or works only in the original private chat. The implementation separates preview/unpackaging from receiving, blocks group contexts, and allows eligible token holders to receive. Explain one recipient and the receiving action. Do not imply stronger recipient protection than the product implements.

giving.giftMessage.body · giving.inTheBag.lead · receiving.refusals.ownGift.line · errors.group_chat
02

Unconfirmed payment is presented as failure

After a lost reply, “チケットが未追加です” states more than the app knows. Use “購入結果を確認できませんでした” and preserve the assurance that checking the same payment will not charge twice. Also change the price comparison from “was / 通常価格” to the cost of buying individual tickets.

tickets.checkout.notAdded.title · tickets.checkout.notAdded.line · tickets.checkout.was
03

Search guidance contradicts search behavior

The no-results paragraph says a username must be exact, but the server searches substrings. It also infers that a named person has not joined and adds a gifting tutorial. Replace it with a neutral no-match state and one short search hint.

explore.search.notFound.title · explore.search.notFound.lead
04

Keep consequences where the action needs them

“Start a new sticker” discards the unsealed sticker and spent ticket. The sealing 18+ switch does not disclose that the mark is permanent, although later marking does. Make those consequences visible. Gratitude’s not-received recovery must ask the person to finish receiving, not to place the sticker on the board.

stickerCreation.seal.startOver · stickerCreation.sealSheet.nsfw · gratitude.refusals.notReceived
05

Expired does not mean already returned

The receiving screen shares copy between an expired gift and one whose return has completed. Before the return job succeeds, “returned” may be false. Use expiry language for the shared state or separate the states.

receiving.refusals.giftReturned.title · receiving.refusals.giftReturned.line · giving.giftBag.stamps.returned.big
06

Explain the short Kyoto Seika exercise without exam equivalence

The current how-to repeats the summary and compares the start to the real test. That comparison can be read as referring only to the start signal, so this is ambiguity rather than a definite mistranslation. State the app’s own timing and retain the university non-affiliation note.

stickerBoard.settings.kyotoSeika.about · stickerBoard.settings.kyotoSeika.how
07

The Japanese Terms body is still English

The Japanese Terms page contains an English parody, and the Privacy pages contain different videos. These are intentional placeholders rather than completed translations. Decide their intended content before any language sign-off. This is a content-parity finding, not a legal assessment.

What supports the Japanese

Source-backed means the named term or interaction pattern is supported by an official Japanese product reference. It does not mean the entire Croquis sentence was quoted from that app. Product glossary means an approved Croquis term. Editorial means a reasoned language recommendation. Custom metaphors such as 仕上げる and 感謝 need consistent product meaning; an unrelated app cannot certify them.

Keep established terms such as LINEでログイン, ユーザー名, 友だち, トーク, and the approved drawing-tool language. Prefer short action labels and polite explanatory sentences. The major language cleanup is literal phrasing such as 何も動いていません, repetitive explanations, and labels that name an implementation step instead of the user’s outcome.

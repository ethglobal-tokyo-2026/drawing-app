Sticker crease follow-ups, reassessed October 12, 2026.

The feature is deployed and remains behind the developer slip's "Show creases on this device",
off by default. The thought trails and overlap-height gloss shipped in c1a6368e.

The current follow-up changes:
- terminate the shared worker when no board shows creases, releasing its decoded mask cache;
- share still-wanted pending jobs across batches and skip completed jobs without retaining their blobs;
- tell the worker about changed keys immediately, before the 90 ms settle delay, so a quick
  change and return cannot leave a sticker waiting for a result the worker thinks it already posted;
- keep each crease in its own composited surface so WebKit does not repaint the overlapping board
  when the directional opacity fades end. The light, tones, fades, and device switch stay the same.

Physical iPhone measurement remains open. The connected phone was found, but Safari refused its
WebDriver session because Web Inspector was disabled. Both Web Inspector and Remote Automation
must be enabled under Settings > Apps > Safari > Advanced, with the phone unlocked.
A desktop WebKit result does not establish actual phone performance.

Current measurements, probe source, screenshots, and the original 32-sticker fixture are retained
locally in data/verification/sticker-crease-2026-10-12/. The fixture's old databases must be copied
for investigation and do not migrate directly through current drawn_width requirements.
The superseded review screenshots, experimental patches, and full reviews remain in Git history.

Sticker crease critique (impeccable critique), 2026-10-09
Board: crease-ann (32 overlapping stickers, 22 creased), a calm board of three one-stroke stickers, phone
390x844 DPR 3 and iPad 1024x1366 DPR 2, Chromium and WebKit. Creases on via localStorage board.creases.
Numbers are luma changes (0-255) between the same frame with creases shown and hidden, from
/tmp/crease-critique/analysis.txt; tuning values are starting points to try, not calibrated.

1. [P1] On a crowded phone board the crease reads as drawn white lines, not pressed ridges.
   Screenshot: critique-1.png (left creases hidden, right shown, light at rest).
   The lit side lands as a thin white line on the sticker's face (+23 to +26 luma at p95 on mid-tone faces,
   up to +37; up to +41 on dark faces), while the shade side barely registers there. A sticker over several
   others carries every silhouette beneath it as a pale outline (the purple blob, the blue cloud, the brown
   bunny), so it looks like a ghost drawing on top of the artist's own strokes. PRODUCT.md: a sticker is cut
   from its artist's own strokes; DESIGN.md: the crease shows edges "as a real one does pressed down".
   Fix: make the lit side a soft ramp instead of a line. In crease.ts try RAMP 4 -> 6 and ROUND 1 -> 2 (wider,
   softer ramp), CREASE_TONE.lit 0.42 -> 0.28 and MOST.lit 0.5 -> 0.32, so the highlight peaks near the
   shade's strength; keep CREASE_TONE.shade and STRENGTH. Recheck against critique-1's purple blob.

2. [P2] A fast move of the light cross-fades the two opposite bakes, so for a moment every edge is drawn white
   and ink at once.
   Screenshot: critique-2.png (crop at rest, 40 ms after the mouse jumps from the top-left to the bottom-right
   corner, then settled).
   facts.json, 55 ms after the jump: topLeft 0.438, bottomRight 0.533, topRight 0.169 (sum 1.14). A slow pass
   fades through the overhead light as designed (sum dips to about 0.22 halfway), but a jump, a pen set down
   elsewhere, or a flick of the phone runs each layer's own 280 ms opacity transition from where it was, so
   the lit and shaded images of the same ramp overlap and the ramp turns grey for a beat.
   Fix: transition the light, not the layers. In sticker-crease.css drop `transition: opacity 280ms` on
   `.sticker-crease > i`; register --lx and --ly with @property as <number> and transition them on
   `.sticker-crease` (about 280ms, --ease-out), so the blend follows the light's path through overhead.
   If @property isn't wanted, shorten the layer transition to 90ms.

Sticker crease critique (impeccable critique), 2026-10-09
Method: dual-agent (A: design review, B: detector), one batched browser round plus one follow-up.
Boards: crease-ann (32 overlapping stickers, 22 creased) and a calm board of three one-stroke stickers;
phone 390x844 DPR 3, iPad 1024x1366 DPR 2; Chromium. Creases on via localStorage board.creases.
Luma changes (0-255) compare the same frame with creases shown and hidden at the same light. Tuning values
are starting points to try, not calibrated. "Computed" means arithmetic from the constants, not measured.

Score (Nielsen, passive material: H3, H5, H6, H7, H9, H10 n/a): H1 3, H2 3, H4 2, H8 3 = 11/16 (Good).
Verdict: specific to Croquis (a stiff sheet draped over the edges beneath, buried edges hidden); where it
fails, it fails on the artist's own art.

1. [P1] The crease reads as strokes drawn on the art, white at rest and ink with the light on the far side.
   Screenshots: critique-1.png (left hidden, right shown, light at rest); critique-3.png (rest, far-side light
   hidden, far-side light shown).
   At rest the lit side is a thin white line on the face (+23 to +26 luma at p95 on mid-tone faces, up to +41
   on dark ones); a sticker over several others carries every silhouette beneath as a pale outline (the
   purple blob, the blue cloud). With the light at the bottom right the same edge turns into a dark arc that
   passes for a pen line. PRODUCT.md: a sticker is cut from its artist's own strokes; DESIGN.md: edges show
   "as a real one does pressed down".
   Fix (crease.ts): soften and lower both sides: RAMP 4 -> 6, ROUND 1 -> 2, CREASE_TONE.lit 0.42 -> 0.28,
   MOST.lit 0.5 -> 0.32, CREASE_TONE.foot 0.2 -> 0.12, MOST.shade 0.3 -> 0.22.

2. [P1] Every crease on the board fades out together when the light passes overhead, and that spot is inside
   ordinary use.
   No screenshot (a frame at the null shows nothing); facts.json slowBRtoTL: the four opacities sum to 0.22
   halfway (bottomRight 0.048, topRight 0.172).
   With --crease-swing 1.5 the null sits at a pointer about 69% across and 77% down, or a phone leaned about
   52 degrees (computed): a pen working the lower right of an iPad board, or a phone held a little upright,
   blanks all 22 creases at once. A real ridge under an overhead light keeps a faint shoulder glint and foot.
   Fix: bake CREASE_TONE.shoulder and .foot into a fifth layer at constant opacity, out of the four directional
   bakes. Quick tune: --crease-swing 1.5 -> 1.1 moves the null to about 76% / 87% (computed).

3. [P2] Off-axis lights show the crease stronger than at rest, against the CSS's own "never stronger than at
   rest".
   Measured total change vs rest: crowded phone board 1.21x (top right) and 1.33x (bottom right), iPad 1.34x
   (top right), calm board 1.92x and 2.01x. Two neighbouring sides' opacities sum to as much as 1.38 (facts.json
   opacity-tr 0.544 + 0.839).
   Fix: the fifth layer in 2 takes the light-independent terms out of the sum; or divide each side's opacity
   by max(1, |a| + |b|) in sticker-crease.css, with |v| written max(v, -1 * v).

4. [P2] A fast move of the light cross-fades the two opposite bakes, so the ramp turns grey for a beat.
   Screenshot: critique-2.png (rest, 40 ms after the mouse jumps from the top-left to the bottom-right corner,
   settled). facts.json at 55 ms: topLeft 0.438, bottomRight 0.533, topRight 0.169.
   A slow pass fades through overhead as designed, but a jump or a pen set down elsewhere runs each layer's own
   280 ms opacity transition from where it was.
   Fix: transition the light, not the layers: drop `transition: opacity 280ms` on `.sticker-crease > i`,
   register --lx and --ly with @property as <number> and transition them on `.sticker-crease`; or cut the layer
   transition to 90ms.

5. [P3] On a white border only the ink half can show, so a crease there is a grey smudge.
   Screenshot: critique-4.png (calm board: hidden, rest, bottom-right light).
   Shade-only on white is physically fair; the foot's ink is what reads as dirt. Fix: the foot cut in 1.

Holds up: only edges directly beneath show (no deeper outlines in the crops); it reads the one light and
honors reduced motion; the slow sweep turns smoothly through all four sides; lifting clears it and a drop
presses it in.

== Split of zipper.ts (1,446 lines; under the ~2,000 limit)

[Z1] Three cuts share no mutable state with createZipper:
  - zipperParts.ts: make, svg, gradient, sliderBody, pullFace (:284-465), ~180 lines of static SVG artwork.
  - zipperMouth.ts: hermite, MouthShape, blankShape, gapOf, fillShape (taking S0, S1, travel as arguments instead of closing over them), mouthRange, showsFrom and their constants (:467-527, :774-817), ~110 lines. trayEngine.ts then imports the mouth's geometry without the Zipper's DOM module.
  - zipperShake.ts: devicemotion to a jolt, gravity smoothing, reduced-motion switching (:1309-1348), ~40 lines, as `listenForJolts(win, reduced, onJolt) => stop`.
  createZipper keeps build, step, render and pointer input, ~1,050 lines. Artwork is the clearest win. Confidence: low-medium (not over the limit).
  Frame cost of the loop, verified by reading: per frame the chain moves, ~50 parted teeth each scan the sample table (P6), ~50 fixed teeth are re-formatted (P1), ~160 slivers do 6 gap() calls each where 3 do (P2), then onFrame runs mouthRange (~200 gap() calls) and rewrites tabindex (P3). All DOM writes are transform/opacity, skipped when unchanged; no layout reads in the loop. timeOurWork costs one null check while the recorder is off.
